Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

1. Overview

Kukku is designed to run AI features primarily on-device. We aim to minimize data collection and keep user content private.

2. Data We Process

Kukku may process text prompts, uploaded documents, selected images, profile details (such as display name/avatar), and optional tool configuration to provide app features.

3. On-Device Processing

By default, core AI inference is performed locally on your device.

4. When Data May Leave Device

If you enable tools or modes that use external services (for example web search or third-party API tools), relevant query data is sent to those providers to return results.

5. Permissions

Depending on features you use, Kukku may request access to camera, microphone/speech recognition, photos, contacts, calendars/reminders, health data, location, and local network discovery.

6. Backups

Backup files may include app settings and agent/tool setup. API keys are redacted from backups and must be re-entered after restore.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is only shared with service providers you explicitly configure through enabled tools/features.

8. Subscription and Monetization Analytics

Kukku may generate subscription and paywall telemetry events to understand onboarding and subscription performance. These events are designed to be privacy-safe and do not include personal content such as prompt text, emails, phone numbers, contacts, or message text.

Data may include anonymous install/session identifiers, app version/build metadata, paywall and subscription event outcomes, product identifiers, optional country/currency metadata, and sanitized error codes/messages. This telemetry is used only for analytics and entitlement reliability, not cross-app tracking.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Most app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete content in-app or uninstall the app to remove local app data, subject to your device backup settings.

10. Children

Kukku is not directed to children under 13.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact support@kukku.me.
